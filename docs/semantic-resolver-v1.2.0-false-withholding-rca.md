# RCA: False withholding in semantic resolver v1.2.0

Analysis only (2026-11-26). The analysis itself changed no code, fixture, decision or report. All counterfactual rule sets were compiled **in memory** with the exported `compileSemanticSnapshot`: they are hypotheses, not changes. The experiment scripts were ad hoc and are not committed; the test plan in §G turns each experiment into a reproducible test. The implementation plan that follows from this RCA is [semantic-resolver-v1.3.0-plan.md](./semantic-resolver-v1.3.0-plan.md).

> **Scope warning.** The 24-example frame is the committed **synthetic demo frame** (`origin: "fixture"`), not historical data. Every figure below describes those 24 synthetic rows only. Nothing here measures production performance, and a 24-row frame cannot establish any target.

## A. Executive summary

- **Cases analysed:** 9.
- **Confirmed causes:** 5 cases (demo-008, 010, 012, 015, 017).
- **Probable causes:** 3 cases (demo-011, 016, 020).
- **Unresolved:** 1 case (demo-019).
- **No resolver implementation defect explains any of the nine.** Every case follows the same path: Gate 0 finds no canonical identity, `normalizeAliasKey` handles the Cyrillic key correctly, the key has no entry in the lookup, and Gate 5 returns the `unresolved_unknown` hard stop. The resolver has no thresholds, fuzzy matching or ranking that could have "almost" matched.
- **The main systemic cause is coverage, not logic.** The code seed is deliberately small. Phase 3 decision 1 (Option B, incremental curation; [phase-3-hybrid-semantic-registry.md:1522](./phase-3-hybrid-semantic-registry.md#L1522)) keeps it small on purpose. It has 37 rules: 36 language-neutral, **1 Russian, 0 Ukrainian**. Of the 97 exact legacy keys that `semanticKeyFor` maps to a manifest concept, the resolver resolves 21 and does not resolve 76. The nine cases are symptoms of two parts of that backlog:
  1. un-migrated English aliases (demo-008, 010, 011);
  2. no Russian or Ukrainian synonyms for concepts that already exist (demo-012, 015, 017).
- **Three cases should not be "fixed" by an alias.**
  - demo-016 and demo-020 carry their meaning in the excerpt or the unit, not in the key. The keys `потолок` ("ceiling") and `двигун` ("engine") are under-specified, and the resolver never receives the excerpt.
  - demo-019 (`дальність`, bare "range") conflicts with the reviewer's own `withhold_context` decision on the equivalent Russian demo-014. The resolver also deliberately leaves English bare `range` unaliased.
- **The most important systemic finding is a latent defect, not one of the nine causes.** `detectLanguage` needs at least two Ukrainian-only letters, so it classifies short Ukrainian excerpts as Russian (demo-018, 019, 020). The fixture frame supplies `language` explicitly and hides this. In the historical frame and in any production path, where language is derived, any `uk`-scoped alias added for v1.3.0 would silently not fire. The evaluation would then overstate Ukrainian coverage.

### C.2 Independent causes vs symptoms of one cause

1. **S1 Curated-seed coverage backlog** (deliberate; explains A-cases 008, 010, 011). The seed migrates legacy keys one by one with a regression test each. Measured: 76 of 97 legacy key→concept mappings are not yet migrated (in-memory comparison against `semanticKeyFor`). The legacy mappings are **not** ground truth: several, such as bare `range`, `mass`, `cost` and `altitude`, are exactly the kind the spec wants re-curated. So this list is a candidate backlog, not a to-do list.
2. **S2 Asymmetric language coverage** (explains B-cases 012, 015, 017; contributes to 016, 020). The seed has 36 `all` rules, 1 `ru` rule and 0 `uk` rules. For 41 of 42 concepts there is no Cyrillic synonym, while English keys found in Russian and Ukrainian text resolve through `all`. This is one shared cause, not three defects.
3. **S3 Key-only resolution with lossy extracted keys** (explains D-cases 016 and 020). The extractor stored a head noun (_потолок_, _двигун_) and dropped the qualifier that carries the meaning (_практический_, _потужність_), with no label. The resolver behaves correctly in withholding. The defect, if any, is upstream: key and label extraction, which is outside the resolver.
4. **S4 Reviewer or concept-boundary uncertainty** (019, partly 011). The `range.max` and `range.operational` descriptions overlap, and two equivalent "flight range" cards received different verdicts.

S1 and S2 are the same underlying fact: the seed is small by design, and nobody has curated non-English synonyms yet. S3 and S4 are independent of it.

### C.3 Latent defects found along the way (not causes of the nine)

- **L1 Ukrainian is misdetected as Russian on short text** (confirmed behaviour). [language.ts:38](../src/entities/normalization/language.ts#L38) requires `count(UKRAINIAN_ONLY) ≥ 2`. "Дальність польоту становить 250 км." and "Потужність двигуна становить 80 кВт." each contain one, so both become `ru`. The sampler uses `row.language ?? detectLanguage(row.excerpt)` ([sampling.ts:232](../src/entities/normalization/semantic-eval/sampling.ts#L232)), and the planned historical frame has no stored language. As a result, `uk`-scoped aliases would not fire for these rows, and the rows would be stratified as Russian. The impact on production is **probable**: the resolver is not integrated in production yet.
- **L2 English short excerpts become `unknown`** (fewer than 6 words). An `en`-scoped alias would then never apply, so English aliases must stay `all`-scoped, as all existing ones are.
- **L3 Unit-suffix stripping is Latin-only.** `TRAILING_UNIT = /_(km|kmh|kg|m)$/` ([:108](../src/entities/normalization/semantic-resolver.ts#L108)), so `дальність_км` and `потолок_м` keep their suffix. Not a cause here (no Cyrillic suffix appears in the frame), but it caps the reach of every Cyrillic alias.

## E. Regression risks: behaviour that must stay unchanged

- **Negative controls stay unresolved:** `total_cost`, `programme_cost`, `program_project_cost`, `project_cost`, `cost_total`, `cost`, `unit_cost_of_nothing`, `zorblax_rating`, `zorblax`, `quantum_deflector_rating`, plus the `UNKNOWN_PROBES` list in [replay.ts:181-204](../src/entities/normalization/semantic-eval/replay.ts#L181-L204) (incl. `масса`, `нагрузка`, `боевые_части`, `левітація`).
- **Class 4 stays ambiguous:** `radius`, `power`, `capacity`.
- **Guards stay intact:** `beam` (Sea only), `weight` (mass unit only).
- **Deliberately unaliased:** bare `range`, `дальность`, `дальність`, `дальность_полета`, `корисне_навантаження` (demo-014 and demo-018 are human `withhold_context` and must stay withheld), bare `engine`/`двигун`, `production_rate`.
- **Language scope:** `боевая_часть` resolves only for `ru`. Every new `ru`/`uk` rule must fail for any other or missing caller language.
- **Distinct pairs:** `MUST_DIFFER` and `MUST_STAY_DISTINCT`: `speed.max` ≠ `speed.cruise`, `range_min` ≠ `range`, the C-UAS target concepts ≠ the vehicle's own.
- **Invariants and existing tests:** INV-01…INV-13 at 100 % (Tier 1), and the existing alias-coverage test file unchanged.

## F. Recommended v1.3.0 scope

**Safe and well supported (one small change):**

1. R1 (`fuel_capacity`, volume-guarded) and R2 (`production_rate_monthly`, `monthly_production`).
2. R3, the Russian part: `максимальная_скорость`, `мощность_двигателя`.
3. Regression tests from §G, written before the rules.

**Needs more evidence or a decision first:**

4. R8 language detection: a separate change by the owner of `language.ts`, with translation-routing tests. **Prerequisite for any `uk` rule.**
5. R3, the Ukrainian part (`максимальна_швидкість`), after R8. R3b only with a reviewed example.
6. R7: adjudication of demo-014 vs demo-019 and a written bare-range policy.
7. R4: wording of `range.max` vs `range.operational`, then the alias.
8. R5: Class 4 `потолок` + `практический_потолок`.

**Defer explicitly:** R6 (`двигун`), R9 (Gate 0 widening), R10 (Cyrillic suffixes), and bulk migration of the 76 legacy keys. Spec decision 1 requires one-by-one curation.

Expected effect, stated honestly: the safe scope turns 4 of the 9 into resolutions (008, 010, 012, 015). It does not address the true problem size, which only a historical frame can show.

## G. Test plan

All tests belong in a new test file plus `replay.ts` probe lists. Do not edit the v1.2.0 fixtures.

- **Positive:** each new rule with its eval key; case and delimiter variants (`Максимальная скорость`, `МАКСИМАЛЬНАЯ-СКОРОСТЬ`); `fuel_capacity` with `l`, `L`, `litres`, `л`.
- **Negative, ambiguous terms:** `capacity`, `power`, `radius`, `потолок` (ambiguous after R5), `скорость`, `мощность`, `дальность`, `дальність`, `двигун`, `production_rate`, `fuel_capacity` with `kg` or no unit.
- **Language and transliteration:** each `ru` rule with `uk`, `en` and no language gives not resolved, and the mirror for `uk`. Transliterated forms (`maksimalnaya_skorost`) stay unknown until explicitly aliased (spec §900). For R8: the 4 short Ukrainian sentences in the frame, Russian sentences with ы/э/ъ/ё, mixed text quoting a Ukrainian name in Russian, and English unchanged.
- **Regression of existing aliases:** the whole `CODE_SEED_RULES` set resolves exactly as in v1.2.0 (snapshot-diff test over every rule key); INV-01…13; existing `semantic-resolver-alias-coverage.test.ts`.
- **Unknown terms:** the `UNKNOWN_PROBES` list plus `quantum_deflector_rating`, `fuel`, `fuel_consumption`, `operating_temperature_range`.
- **Unrelated concepts:** `максимальная_скорость_цели` must not become `speed.max` (C-UAS target speed); `мощность_передатчика` must not become `power.engine`; `range_operational`/`operating_range_min` must not become `range.operational`.

## H. How to compare v1.3.0 with the frozen v1.2.0 baseline
